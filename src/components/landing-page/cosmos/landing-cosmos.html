Chapter 06 — The intelligence layer

One system. One context. Intelligence that grows with your startup.

Most AI tools meet your startup one prompt at a time. SFCollab is designed to understand the wider journey.

Because the Vision, team, documents, meetings, contributions, startup materials, decisions, and execution can live inside one connected ecosystem, the AI assistant can work with far more meaningful context. Instead of repeatedly explaining what the startup does, who is responsible for what, which decisions were made, what the current priorities are, and which files matter — the system gradually builds a more continuous understanding of the project.

Built toward persistent organizational intelligence: designed to preserve more project context, reduce the need to repeatedly explain your startup, and help prevent knowledge from becoming fragmented.
